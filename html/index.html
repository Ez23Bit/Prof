Project 1: Mobile based Reservation System

NU Reserve is a campus facility reservation system built for National University. It lets students book facilities such as the Court, computer laboratories, classrooms, and the Function Hall through a mobile application, instead of doing it manually.


Project 2: Tung Tung Turon

Tung Tung Turon is a two-page promotional website for a small-batch Filipino snack business that sells turon, a crispy fried banana and jackfruit roll coated in caramelized sugar. The site promotes the product through photos of the actual turon and a warm banana-leaf green and caramel gold color scheme. It is fully responsive, so it works on both desktop and mobile.
